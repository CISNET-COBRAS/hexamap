# hexamap: Hexagonal Heatmaps for Age-Period-Cohort Data

Visualize age-period-cohort (APC) data with hexamaps, a field of
hexagonal tiles that places the age, period and cohort axes at equal 60
degree angles. Unlike the traditional Lexis diagram, a hexamap does not
compress patterns between adjacent cohorts or stretch patterns within a
cohort, and adjacent tiles along every axis share a full edge.
Implements the method described in Jalal and Burke (2020)
[doi:10.1097/EDE.0000000000001236](https://doi.org/10.1097/EDE.0000000000001236)
.
